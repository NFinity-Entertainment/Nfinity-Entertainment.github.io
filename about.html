Our Journey

NFinity Entertainment is an independent global entertainment label and artist management agency founded in 2026 by CheriS member Nana (NF). Built around the concept of limitless artistic expression, the agency oversees music production, visual branding, and global promotions for its roster.

Our Founder

Nana of CheriS founded Nfinity on November 10 2026 with the hopes of supporting independent artists to ensure they reach their full potential. She is the current center, leader, and main vocalist of her group and is the eldest member so she often gets refferred to as Nana Unnie.

Our Mission

Our goal here at Nfinity is to support young independent artists to help them unlock their limitless potential. Our logo symbolizes infinte possibilites for growth and success because we believe that anyone should be able to have a chance at becoming an idol as long as they are willing to put in the effort.
